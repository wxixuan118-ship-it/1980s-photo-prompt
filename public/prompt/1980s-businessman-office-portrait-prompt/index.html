1980s Businessman Office Portrait Prompt

Wood-Panelled Office Man · the full 1980s businessman office portrait prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

The early-eighties office had a look all its own: panelled walls, a heavy desk, a tie wide enough to be a statement. Seeing yourself as the manager in that room is funny and oddly dignified at the same time. It is a favourite for people who work in offices today and want to compare their desk with their parents' generation.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, place me as a man at work in a 1983 corporate office. Preserve my face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions exactly and keep me instantly recognisable; do not retouch or idealise me. Give me neatly combed hair with a side part and a charcoal pinstripe suit with a pale blue shirt and a wide burgundy paisley tie. Seat me behind a heavy oak desk in a wood-panelled office with a rotary desk phone, a Rolodex, a brass desk lamp, stacked manila folders and a glass ashtray, venetian blinds behind me. I am looking up from paperwork toward the camera, pen in hand. Overhead fluorescent tubes mixed with window light give a slight green-yellow cast. Shot on 35mm colour film for a company newsletter: medium grain, muted contrast, mild softness. No later-decade computers, no modern text or logos; a real photograph, not a filter or illustration.

What this prompt creates

A mid-shot of you seated behind a heavy oak desk in a wood-panelled office, dressed in a charcoal pinstripe suit, pale blue shirt and wide burgundy paisley tie. Around you are a rotary phone, a Rolodex, a brass lamp, manila folders and a glass ashtray, with venetian blinds behind. The light has a faint green-yellow fluorescent tint, and the image has the muted contrast and grain of a newsletter photo.

How to use this 1980s businessman office portrait prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the suit colour, tie pattern and desk props to reflect your own job, such as blueprints for an engineer or ledgers for an accountant.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a photo where you are looking at the camera with a neutral or slight smile; the "looking up from paperwork" pose reads best with a direct gaze.
  • If the tie or suit comes out too modern and slim, add "wide lapels and a wide tie, early-1980s cut" and regenerate the same chat.
  • Replace the paisley tie with a knitted or striped one, or swap the ashtray for a coffee mug and a calculator for a less smoky office.

Who this prompt suits

Office workers, managers and anyone who enjoys workplace humour; it works well on LinkedIn as a light-hearted post, in team chats, or as a retro profile picture for a work anniversary.

What makes this prompt work

"Do not retouch or idealise me" keeps your real face instead of a generic executive. The desk props, "a rotary desk phone, a Rolodex ... a glass ashtray", are specific enough to date the room instantly. "Overhead fluorescent tubes mixed with window light" produces the slightly off colour real office photos had, and "for a company newsletter" sets a modest, documentary tone. "No later-decade computers" stops anachronistic screens appearing on the desk.
