1980s Aerobics Class Cartoon Prompt

Aerobics Class With Cake · the full 1980s aerobics class cartoon prompt, ready to paste into Seedream or any other image generator you use.

The joke is instantly readable: four people giving an aerobics routine everything they have while each carefully balances a plate of layered cake. The pastel pink and turquoise studio, neon shorts, leg warmers and sweatbands are rendered with a clean, glossy illustration style that feels like the cover of a VHS workout tape crossed with a birthday card.

Best with: Seedream

Prompt

Retro 80s/90s fitness class scene with bright neon colors and a fun, energetic vibe. Four people are working out in a pastel-colored exercise studio with plain walls (no mirrors, no reflections anywhere):

1. Blonde man in bright workout shorts and a headband


2. Dark-haired man in 80s track pants and a sleeveless tank


3. Blonde woman with big, voluminous 80s hair, leg warmers, and a flashy crop top


4. Hispanic woman with big 80s hair, colorful spandex, and wristbands



Each person is holding a slice of cake in one hand while doing upbeat aerobics or dance moves. Background is simple pastel studio walls and floor, no mirror surfaces. Vibe is goofy, nostalgic, and high-energy, like an 80s workout tape cover. Vibrant digital illustration, clean and bold.
Illustration & 3D Fashion & Hair

What this prompt creates

A bright digital illustration of four dancers mid-step across a shiny pink floor. A shirtless blond man wears lime shorts and a headband, a dark-haired man wears striped track pants, a blonde woman sports a sequin crop top and pink leg warmers, and a woman with a curly black mane wears swirling spandex. Each holds a cake slice aloft. Ballet barres run along pastel walls and a mirror panel appears at the left edge.

How to use this 1980s aerobics class cartoon prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open Seedream, or any image generator you prefer, and paste the prompt into a new chat or prompt box.
  3. Swap the cake slices for donuts, pizza or smartphones and change the four outfits to match your own friends or team.
  4. Generate three or four versions and keep the one with the most convincing grain, color and light. Use the tips below if the result looks too modern.

Tips for this prompt

  • Add 'VHS cover typography space at top' or a title like 'Sweat and Sweets' to turn the image into a ready-made mock workout tape.
  • If the mirror keeps appearing, describe the walls as 'solid painted brick' and move the barres out of the prompt entirely.
  • For a photo instead, replace the illustration line with 'flash photo, 1985 home video still, soft grain' and keep the outfits.

Who this prompt suits

Fun for bakery or café social posts, gym promotions with a sense of humour, retro party invites, birthday cards and anyone making nostalgic comedy graphics or merch.

What makes this prompt work

Numbering the four characters gives each one a distinct outfit, so the model avoids clones and produces a varied lineup. Details like "leg warmers", "wristbands" and "big, voluminous 80s hair" are shorthand for the era. "Holding a slice of cake in one hand" supplies the absurd hook. The prompt repeats "no mirrors" several times, yet one still slipped in at the edge, showing how stubbornly the model links dance studios with mirrors.
