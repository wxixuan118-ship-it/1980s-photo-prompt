1980s Yearbook Page Collage Prompt

Yearbook Page Collage With Classmates · the full 1980s yearbook page collage prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

By Vivian Wang · Updated Sep 28, 2026 · How we pick and check prompts

A yearbook page says more about the decade than any single portrait: the stiff poses, the uneven hair, the doodles someone added in ballpoint. Seeing yourself as one small tile among a class of strangers is funny and oddly convincing, and it makes a great alternative to the usual single-headshot throwback edit.

Best with: ChatGPT

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, create a full 1980s high-school yearbook page in which my portrait is one tile in a grid of twelve small school photos. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age and proportions clearly recognisable; do not beautify or smooth my skin. In my tile I wear a wide-collared striped shirt with a thin sweater vest, big layered hair and a slightly nervous half-smile against a speckled grey school backdrop. The other eleven tiles are clearly different fictional classmates with braces, oversized frames and side ponytails. Set the grid on blue-lined notebook paper with pink highlighter stripes, ballpoint stars, arrows and a doodled cassette. Under each tile put short squiggly handwriting that is only illegible scribbles, never real names. Show halftone dots, faded ink and a yellowed edge. No real names, brands, logos or modern objects; a scanned printed page, not a digital graphic or illustration. Make the person from my uploaded photo the large central portrait on the page, clearly recognisable, with the classmates as smaller tiles around it.

What this prompt creates

The edited image looks like a scanned yearbook page. Your school portrait sits in a grid of twelve small tiles, surrounded by fictional classmates with braces, big frames and side ponytails. Behind the grid is blue-lined notebook paper with pink highlighter stripes, stars, arrows and a doodled cassette. Captions are loose scribbles, and the print shows halftone dots, faded ink and a yellowed edge.

How to use this 1980s yearbook page collage prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change your tile's outfit, the paper colour and the doodle theme, for example band sketches, sports pennants or hearts, to match your own school memories.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a straight-on, evenly lit headshot; the tile is small, so a clear face with visible eyes survives the downscale much better than a candid.
  • If classmates start resembling you, reply "make every other tile a distinctly different person in age, face shape and hair" and regenerate the page.
  • For a sports-page variant, ask for team uniforms in the other tiles, or change the doodles to hearts and signatures for a signed-copy look.

Who this prompt suits

Anyone doing a class reunion post, friends swapping throwback edits, teachers making a fun staff intro, or creators who want a nostalgic carousel image that is more playful than a single portrait.

What makes this prompt work

"My portrait is one tile in a grid of twelve" tells the model which face must stay yours while the rest are invented. "Clearly different fictional classmates" stops it from cloning your face across the page. "Only illegible scribbles, never real names" avoids fake text and real identities, while "halftone dots" and "a yellowed edge" make it read as printed paper rather than "a digital graphic or illustration".
