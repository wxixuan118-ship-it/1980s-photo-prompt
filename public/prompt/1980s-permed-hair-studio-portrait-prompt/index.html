1980s Permed Hair Studio Portrait Prompt

Permed Hair Studio Glamour Portrait · the full 1980s permed hair studio portrait prompt, ready to paste into Gemini (Nano Banana) or any other image generator you use.

By Vivian Wang · Updated Sep 28, 2026 · How we pick and check prompts

This could pass for a 1985 department-store glamour sitting. The curly perm is huge, the blue-to-pink eyeshadow and blush are laid on without apology, and the mottled blue backdrop is the exact painted canvas every portrait studio owned. Oversized pink-tinted glasses, which the model added on its own, finish the look perfectly.

Best with: Gemini (Nano Banana)

Prompt

A photorealistic feminine photo in the style of 1985. The subject has voluminous, permed hair with a lot of height, a classic mid-80s style. She is wearing bold 1980s makeup, including bright blue and pink eyeshadow, heavy pink blush, and vibrant magenta lipstick. She is dressed in a low-cut silk blouse. The background is a soft-focus studio backdrop in muted blue and gray tones with professional studio lighting that highlights the texture of her hair.
Portrait Fashion & Hair

What this prompt creates

A realistic head-and-shoulders studio portrait of a woman with a dense, voluminous dark curly perm, large translucent pink frames, bright blue and pink eyeshadow, strong rosy blush and glossy magenta lipstick. She wears an emerald-green silk blouse with a deep V neckline and turns slightly toward the camera. Soft, even studio light shows the hair texture against a blurred blue-grey mottled backdrop.

How to use this 1980s permed hair studio portrait prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open Gemini (Nano Banana), or any image generator you prefer, and paste the prompt into a new chat or prompt box.
  3. Change the hair colour, blouse colour and eyeshadow shades to suit your subject while keeping the 1985 date, the perm and the blue-grey studio backdrop.
  4. Generate three or four versions and keep the one with the most convincing grain, color and light. Use the tips below if the result looks too modern.

Tips for this prompt

  • Specify "no glasses" or "oversized pink-tinted frames" explicitly, since the model may add eyewear on its own and you may want control either way.
  • Add "soft-focus diffusion filter, slight halation, Kodak Portra" for the dreamy glow typical of glamour-studio portraits of the time.
  • Change the blouse to "shoulder-padded blazer" or "off-shoulder sweatshirt" to shift from glamour sitting to power-dressing or aerobics-era looks.

Who this prompt suits

Retro makeover content, glamour-shot or yearbook parody posts, costume and makeup artists building 80s references, and casting or look-book decks for period film and TV productions.

What makes this prompt work

Anchoring the date with "in the style of 1985" and "classic mid-80s style" steers hair, makeup and grading together. Listing the makeup colour by colour, "bright blue and pink eyeshadow, heavy pink blush, and vibrant magenta lipstick", avoids a modern natural-beauty default. "Soft-focus studio backdrop in muted blue and gray tones" recreates the classic painted muslin, and "professional studio lighting that highlights the texture of her hair" keeps the perm crisp.
