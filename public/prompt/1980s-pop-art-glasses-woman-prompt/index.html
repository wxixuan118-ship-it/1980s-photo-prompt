1980s Pop Art Glasses Woman Prompt

Purple Glasses Pop Art Woman · the full 1980s pop art glasses woman prompt, ready to paste into Flux or any other image generator you use.

Strong ink lines, a tightly controlled purple and turquoise palette and a dotted halftone backdrop give this portrait instant graphic punch. The oversized purple frames, matching lipstick and turtleneck make the colour story feel deliberate, and the turquoise earrings echo her eyes. It sits somewhere between a comic panel and an 80s magazine illustration of a confident professional woman.

Best with: Flux

Prompt

This is a digital drawing in a comic book style, featuring a close-up portrait of a woman with a confident expression. She has light blonde, wavy hair that falls just above her shoulders. Her skin is fair, and she has prominent, arched eyebrows. She wears large, rectangular, purple-framed glasses that cover her bright blue eyes. Her makeup is bold, with dark purple lipstick and subtle eyeliner that accentuates her eyes. She is dressed in a high-neck, purple turtleneck sweater. Her earrings are large, teardrop-shaped, and turquoise, matching the color of her eyes. The background is a gradient from deep purple on the left to turquoise on the right, with a textured, halftone pattern that adds depth to the image. The overall style is reminiscent of 1980s pop art, with strong lines and vibrant colors. The image focuses on the woman's face and upper shoulders, capturing her stylish and confident appearance.
Illustration & 3D Portrait

What this prompt creates

A front-facing close-up illustration of a woman with wavy, jaw-length blonde hair, heavy arched brows and large purple cat-eye glasses over turquoise eyes. She wears dark purple lipstick, a ribbed purple turtleneck and teardrop turquoise earrings. Flat colour and clean black outlines define her face, while the background fades from deep violet to teal with scattered halftone dots. The framing crops at the shoulders and centres her gaze directly on the viewer.

How to use this 1980s pop art glasses woman prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open Flux, or any image generator you prefer, and paste the prompt into a new chat or prompt box.
  3. Change the hair colour, the frame shape and the two-colour palette to match yourself or your brand while keeping the halftone comic treatment.
  4. Generate three or four versions and keep the one with the most convincing grain, color and light. Use the tips below if the result looks too modern.

Tips for this prompt

  • Add a speech bubble with a short line of dialogue to lean fully into comic-panel pop art in the style of vintage romance comics.
  • Swap the purple and turquoise pair for hot pink and yellow or red and cyan and keep the same repetition across glasses, lips and clothing.
  • Request 'shoulder pads and a power blazer' instead of the turtleneck to make the 80s office-era styling more obvious.

Who this prompt suits

Handy for social media avatars, optician or eyewear promotions, blog author portraits, stickers and prints, and designers building a bold, limited-palette brand illustration set for print or web.

What makes this prompt work

The prompt is really a colour brief. Repeating purple across "rectangular, purple-framed glasses", "dark purple lipstick" and "purple turtleneck" makes the palette cohesive, while "turquoise, matching the color of her eyes" supplies the single contrast colour. "Gradient from deep purple on the left to turquoise on the right" sets the backdrop, and "textured, halftone pattern" delivers the print-comic feel. "Reminiscent of 1980s pop art" nudges line weight and flat shading.
