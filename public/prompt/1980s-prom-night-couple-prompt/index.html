1980s Prom Night Couple Prompt

Balloon Arch Prom Night · the full 1980s prom night couple prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

The prom portrait under a balloon arch is a rite-of-passage photo, and the mid-eighties versions are gloriously over the top. Ruffles, puffed sleeves and a pastel tux make it one of the most fun couple edits to try. Couples enjoy it because it gives them the prom photo they never had together.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using the uploaded photo(s), show both of us as a couple at a 1985 high-school prom. Keep every person in the photo and preserve each face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions exactly and recognisably; no beautification, de-aging or face swapping. Dress one partner in a powder-blue tuxedo with a ruffled-front shirt, velvet bow tie and matching cummerbund, and the other in a taffeta dress with big puffed sleeves and a sweetheart neckline, a wrist corsage and teased, curled hair, adapting the outfits to each person. Pose us in the classic prom stance, one standing behind the other with hands clasped at the waist, before a balloon arch and a glittering foil curtain. Lit by direct studio flash with a small umbrella fill, shot on medium-format colour film: crisp but grainy, slight magenta cast, glossy print with a thin white border. No modern outfits, phones, text or logos; a real prom portrait, not a filter or illustration.

What this prompt creates

A posed prom portrait of the two of you, one standing behind the other with hands clasped at the waist. One wears a powder-blue tux with a ruffled shirt, velvet bow tie and cummerbund; the other a taffeta dress with puffed sleeves, a corsage and teased curls. Behind you are a balloon arch and a glittering foil curtain, lit by direct flash with a slight magenta cast and a white print border.

How to use this 1980s prom night couple prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the tux and dress colours, the backdrop theme and the prom year to match your own school or anniversary.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a photo where you are standing close together and both faces are sharp; group shots with other people make it harder to keep the right faces.
  • If one face looks younger or different, reply "keep both faces at our real ages exactly as in the photo" and regenerate.
  • Change the tux to white or burgundy and the dress to a sequinned or ruffled tier style to match your real prom colours.

Who this prompt suits

Couples who want a playful anniversary post, friends going as prom dates, and anyone who missed their own prom; it is a strong fit for Instagram, Facebook and wedding-anniversary slideshows.

What makes this prompt work

"No beautification, de-aging or face swapping" matters here because a school prom setting nudges the model toward younger faces. "The classic prom stance, one standing behind the other" gives a precise, familiar pose instead of a random embrace. "Direct studio flash with a small umbrella fill" matches the portable setups used at school dances, and "glossy print with a thin white border" makes it look like a picture from the photo package.
