1980s Warehouse Rave Photo Prompt

Underground Warehouse Rave Flash · the full 1980s warehouse rave photo prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

By Vivian Wang · Updated Sep 28, 2026 · How we pick and check prompts

Late-decade warehouse parties produced some of the most striking snapshots of the era: one flash-lit face in front of a sea of haze, lasers and raised hands. The look is raw and a little blurry, which is exactly why it feels authentic and stands out from the neat studio edits that dominate the trend.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, put me in a late-1980s underground warehouse rave at around 3 a.m. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions clearly recognisable; do not beautify or smooth my skin, and keep my own expression readable. I am mid-dance with one arm raised, sweat on my forehead, in an oversized plain white tee, baggy striped dungarees with one strap hanging and a pushed-back bucket hat. Behind me the crowd is a dark blur of raised hands in haze, cut by a green laser line, a strobe mid-flash and bare bulbs on brick pillars. Shot on a disposable camera with on-camera flash: my face slightly overexposed, heavy grain, a greenish cast and a soft light leak on one side. No modern phones, LED wristbands, logos or readable text; a real rave snapshot, not a filter or illustration.

What this prompt creates

The edited photo shows you mid-dance with one arm up, sweaty and half-smiling, in an oversized white tee, striped dungarees with a loose strap and a pushed-back bucket hat. Behind you a dark crowd of raised hands dissolves into haze under a green laser line, a strobe flash and bare bulbs on brick pillars. The flash overexposes your face, the grain is heavy and a light leak creeps in.

How to use this 1980s warehouse rave photo prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the outfit, the laser colour and your pose, from arm raised to hands on your knees, to match your own dance-floor style.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Choose an upload where your face is fully lit and not turned; the dark scene and flash make weakly lit faces the first thing to change.
  • If the model closes your eyes too much or warps your face, ask for "eyes open, looking just past the camera" and regenerate.
  • For a cooler variant, change the green laser to a red one and the dungarees to a plain tie-dye long-sleeve.

Who this prompt suits

Music fans, DJs, nightlife creators and anyone who wants an edgier throwback for an event promo, a story post or a profile picture that feels like a found party print.

What makes this prompt work

"Keep my own expression readable" matters because dance poses tend to distort faces; it keeps the identity lock working mid-movement. "The crowd is a dark blur of raised hands" avoids sharp invented strangers. "Disposable camera with on-camera flash" and "a greenish cast and a soft light leak" set a cheap, imperfect capture, while ruling out "LED wristbands" removes the most common anachronism in rave edits.
