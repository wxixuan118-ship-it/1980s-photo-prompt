1980s Storybook Illustration Prompt

Eastern European Storybook Portrait · the full 1980s storybook illustration prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

Children's books printed in Eastern Europe in the 1980s had a strange charm: wobbly ink, odd proportions, muted colours and characters who looked slightly worried. Seeing yourself drawn that way is funny and oddly touching, and it offers a break from the realistic photo edits most people are posting in the 1980s trend.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, redraw me as a character in a quirky 1980s Eastern European children's book illustration. This should be a drawing, not a photograph, but I must still be recognisable: keep my face shape, hairstyle, skin tone, eyes, nose, glasses or facial hair if I have them, and my general build, exaggerated gently as a caricature rather than replaced. Draw with thin, slightly trembling black ink lines, long awkward limbs, a solemn puzzled expression. Colour with flat gouache and watercolour washes in muted brick red, mustard, olive and dusty turquoise, with uneven edges and areas left unpainted. Show me walking a small dog past a lopsided tram stop and a leaning lamppost, with one bird watching. Print it on faded, slightly yellowed off-white paper with soft halftone texture, mild colour misregistration. No text, no speech bubbles, no modern objects or logos, not glossy digital art, not a 3D render.

What this prompt creates

This one is an illustration, not a photo. You appear as a lanky, gently caricatured figure walking a small dog past a crooked tram stop and lamppost. Your face shape, hair and any glasses remain recognisable. Lines are thin and shaky, colours are flat washes of brick red, mustard, olive and dusty turquoise, and the page looks like a faded old print with halftone dots and slightly misaligned colour.

How to use this 1980s storybook illustration prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the scene, the animal companion and the four-colour palette to build your own imagined storybook page.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a clear photo with a distinctive feature visible, such as glasses, a fringe or a beard, since those details carry your likeness in a caricature.
  • If the drawing stops looking like you, reply "keep my exact hairstyle and face shape from the upload, less exaggeration" and regenerate.
  • Replace the dog and tram stop with a bicycle and a bakery, or a cat on a fence, to create a different page from the same book.

Who this prompt suits

People who like illustration and gentle humour more than realistic nostalgia; it makes a distinctive profile picture, a card for a friend or a playful contrast in a trend carousel.

What makes this prompt work

"This should be a drawing, not a photograph, but I must still be recognisable" sets the honest goal, and listing "face shape, hairstyle, skin tone, eyes, nose" tells the model which traits to carry into caricature. "Slightly trembling black ink lines" and "areas left unpainted" produce the hand-made look. "Colour misregistration" and "yellowed off-white paper" mimic cheap print runs, while "not glossy digital art" blocks the smooth vector finish models often default to.
