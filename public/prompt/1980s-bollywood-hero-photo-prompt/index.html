1980s Bollywood Hero Photo Prompt

Bollywood Hero Studio Portrait · the full 1980s bollywood hero photo prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

By Vivian Wang · Updated Oct 6, 2026 · How we pick and check prompts

The 1980s Hindi film hero had swagger built from simple things: a blow-dried mane, a proud moustache, an open wide collar and trousers pulled high. Studio portraits against painted scenery were everywhere, from film offices to neighbourhood photo shops, so the look feels both heroic and familiar when your own face is in it.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, turn me into a 1980s Hindi film hero posing for a studio portrait. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age, build and body proportions recognisable and unchanged; do not add muscle or smooth my skin. Give me thick, blow-dried hair with volume at the front and slightly long sideburns, plus a full moustache that suits my face. Dress me in a cream shirt with a wide pointed collar worn open two buttons, a thin gold chain, a wide leather belt and high-waisted brown pleated trousers. Pose me standing with one hand on my hip, body angled, eyes to the lens, in front of a hand-painted studio backdrop of hills and a lake. Light it with a warm tungsten key from the side and soft fill, shot on 35mm colour film with moderate grain, warm yellow cast and a slightly soft focus. No modern objects, text or logos, not a filter, not a painting.

What this prompt creates

The edit is a three-quarter-length studio portrait of you standing with a hand on your hip in a cream wide-collar shirt, a thin gold chain and high-waisted brown trousers. Your hair has volume at the front and longer sideburns, with a full moustache. Behind you is a painted backdrop of hills and a lake, lit by warm tungsten light that gives the whole frame a yellow tint and gentle grain.

How to use this 1980s bollywood hero photo prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Replace the shirt colour, the backdrop scenery and the pose with details you remember from your family's old studio photos.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Use a photo where you are standing or at least shot from the chest up, so the model has a sense of your real build and shoulders.
  • If you already have a beard or dislike moustaches, replace that line with your own facial hair so the face drifts less from the original.
  • For a rougher angry-young-man mood, change the cream shirt to a denim jacket over a black vest and the backdrop to a plain grey wall.

Who this prompt suits

Men who want a playful filmi portrait for Instagram, a dad-and-son comparison post, a groom's pre-wedding joke card, or a profile picture that gets a reaction from the family group chat.

What makes this prompt work

"Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age, build" holds your identity, and "do not add muscle or smooth my skin" blocks the usual action-star makeover. The "hand-painted studio backdrop of hills and a lake" places it in a real photo studio rather than a film set. "Warm tungsten key" and "35mm colour film with moderate grain" set the colour and texture, while "not a filter, not a painting" keeps it looking photographed.
