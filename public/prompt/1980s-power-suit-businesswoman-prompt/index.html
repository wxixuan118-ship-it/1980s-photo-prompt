1980s Power Suit Businesswoman Prompt

Power Suit Businesswoman · the full 1980s power suit businesswoman prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

The power suit is the decade's clearest symbol of ambition: huge shoulders, bold colour and a corner office. Putting yourself into that frame is empowering and a little funny, especially next to the chunky computer. Professional women like this look because it celebrates career confidence in a way that still feels stylish today.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, show me as a woman executive in a high-rise office in 1987. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions unchanged and recognisable; do not smooth, slim or beautify me. Give me a sleek, voluminous blow-dried bob, bold red lipstick and gold clip-on earrings. Dress me in a double-breasted cobalt-blue blazer with broad padded shoulders, a cream blouse and a slim black skirt. I am standing at a floor-to-ceiling window overlooking a city skyline, one hand on a desk that holds a beige boxy computer with a glowing green monitor, a corded push-button phone, a leather briefcase. Mixed light: cool daylight from the window and a warm desk lamp on my face. Shot on 35mm colour film for a business magazine profile: fine grain, slight blue cast in the shadows, mild softness. No flat screens, smartphones, glass-tower skylines, readable text or logos; a real photograph, not a filter or illustration.

What this prompt creates

A mid-length photo of you standing by a floor-to-ceiling office window over a city skyline, in a cobalt-blue double-breasted blazer with broad shoulders, cream blouse and slim skirt, with a blow-dried bob and red lipstick. Your hand rests on a desk holding a beige boxy computer with a green-glowing screen, a push-button phone and a briefcase. Cool window light meets a warm lamp, with fine grain and slight softness.

How to use this 1980s power suit businesswoman prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the blazer colour and pattern, the desk props, or the city view to reflect your own industry and hometown.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a waist-up photo with good posture; standing shots help the model build a convincing silhouette for the padded blazer.
  • If your face shifts, reply "keep my exact face from the uploaded photo, change only outfit and office" and regenerate.
  • Change the blazer to red or houndstooth, or seat yourself at the desk with a fax machine and a coffee cup, for more variations.

Who this prompt suits

Working women, founders and managers, plus anyone marking a promotion or International Women's Day; it suits LinkedIn, Instagram and team chats as a confident, light-hearted post.

What makes this prompt work

"Do not smooth, slim or beautify me" prevents the corporate-headshot polish models tend to add. "Broad padded shoulders" and "double-breasted" force the unmistakable silhouette instead of a modern slim blazer. "Beige boxy computer with a glowing green monitor" dates the office at a glance, and "mixed light" gives realistic uneven colour. "No flat screens ... glass-tower skylines" stops the model drawing a present-day city behind you.
