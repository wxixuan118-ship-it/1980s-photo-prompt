1980s Leather Jacket Motorcycle Prompt

Leather Jacket Motorcycle Road · the full 1980s leather jacket motorcycle prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

By Vivian Wang · Updated Oct 6, 2026 · How we pick and check prompts

A leather jacket, a motorcycle and an empty road at sunset is the decade's idea of freedom in one frame. It is a confident, cinematic look that still feels like a real roadside photo rather than a movie poster. People like it because it shows a tougher, more adventurous side of themselves than a studio portrait.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, show me as a woman standing beside a motorcycle on an empty two-lane highway in 1986. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions exactly and recognisably mine; no beautification or reshaping. Give me big, wind-tousled hair with volume at the roots, and dress me in a black leather jacket with zippers and epaulettes, a white tank top, stonewashed jeans and scuffed ankle boots, with dark aviator sunglasses on. I am leaning on a chrome-heavy standard motorcycle, arms folded, with desert scrub and wooden telephone poles stretching to the horizon. Golden-hour sun low behind me gives a warm rim light and a little lens flare. Shot on 35mm slide film: rich saturated oranges, deep shadows, fine grain, slightly soft corners, an orange date stamp in one corner. No modern motorcycles, helmets, text or logos; a real photograph, not a filter or illustration.

What this prompt creates

A golden-hour roadside photo of you leaning on a chrome motorcycle with arms folded, wearing aviator sunglasses, a zipped black leather jacket, white tank top, stonewashed jeans and ankle boots. Your hair is big and wind-tousled. Desert scrub and telephone poles run to the horizon, the low sun gives a warm rim and small flare, and the colours are rich and saturated like slide film.

How to use this 1980s leather jacket motorcycle prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Change the jacket colour, the type of landscape and the time of day to match a road or place you actually know.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Because sunglasses cover the eyes, upload a clear photo where your jawline, nose and mouth are well lit, so the face stays recognisable.
  • If your face drifts, ask for the sunglasses to be pushed up on your head for one version, then regenerate with them on.
  • Switch to a red leather jacket or a bomber, or change the scene to a neon-lit roadside diner at dusk for a night version.

Who this prompt suits

Women who want a bold, confident look, riders and road-trip lovers, or anyone who grew up on action films; it fits Instagram posts, profile pictures, travel accounts and throwback carousels.

What makes this prompt work

"No beautification or reshaping" keeps your face and build true even with a glamorous setting. "Golden-hour sun low behind me" plus "a little lens flare" gives backlight that feels physical. "35mm slide film" is a specific choice that explains the deep saturated oranges and dark shadows, and "slightly soft corners" adds lens imperfection. "No modern motorcycles, helmets" prevents sleek sport bikes from replacing the classic chrome machine.
