1980s Miami Pastel Sunset Prompt

Pastel Beachfront Sunset Portrait · the full 1980s miami pastel sunset prompt. Upload a photo of yourself to ChatGPT or Gemini, paste this prompt and get your 80s version.

Pastel suits, palm trees and a pink-and-teal sky are some of the most recognisable colours of the decade. This look is popular because it is flattering and warm rather than silly, and it gives your photo a holiday feeling. It also works well next to other posts in the trend, where darker studio shots dominate.

Best with: ChatGPT or Gemini

Prompt

Using my uploaded photo, show me on a 1980s Miami-style beachfront promenade at sunset. Keep my face, facial features, skin tone, age and body proportions faithful and recognisable; do not slim, tan or beautify me. Dress me in either an unstructured pastel linen suit in mint or peach with a plain T-shirt underneath and sleeves pushed up, or a pastel swimsuit with a sheer printed cover-up, plus thin-framed sunglasses pushed up onto my head. Hair is sun-lifted and slightly wind-blown. Behind me stand tall palm trees and a low art deco hotel with rounded corners, under a sky fading from flamingo pink into teal. Warm, low sun lights my face from the side. Shot on 35mm slide film with a slight red-magenta cast, soft grain, mild halation around the highlights and a few dust specks. No readable signs, no modern cars or phones, no logos; a real vacation photograph, not a poster or illustration.

What this prompt creates

You stand on a seaside promenade at golden hour, either in a loose mint or peach linen suit with sleeves pushed up, or a pastel swimsuit with a sheer cover-up. Sunglasses sit on your head and your hair looks lifted by the breeze. Palms and a rounded art deco hotel rise behind you under a flamingo-pink to teal sky, with slide-film colour, gentle halation and faint dust.

How to use this 1980s miami pastel sunset prompt

  1. Tap Copy prompt above to copy the full text.
  2. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a new chat. Upload a clear, well-lit photo where your face is fully visible (no sunglasses or heavy filters); for couple or family prompts, upload a photo with everyone in it.
  3. Paste the prompt and send it. Pick one outfit, choose the pastel colour, and change the hotel or palms to a beach setting you know.
  4. If the face changes, reply “keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photo” and regenerate. Try two or three versions and keep the one that looks most like a real old print.

Tips for this prompt

  • Upload a daylight photo taken outdoors if you can; indoor lighting makes the model work harder to relight your face for a sunset.
  • Delete whichever outfit option you do not want before sending, otherwise the model may blend the suit and the swimwear oddly.
  • For variation, ask for a white convertible parked behind you, or move the scene to a pier with the sun touching the water.

Who this prompt suits

Anyone after a sunny, colourful holiday-style portrait, couples who want a matching pair of shots, and people posting summer throwbacks or travel-themed profile pictures during the trend.

What makes this prompt work

"Do not slim, tan or beautify me" adds "tan" because sunny beach scenes often darken or change skin tone. Offering "either" a suit or a swimsuit with cover-up lets you delete one option for a clear outfit. "A sky fading from flamingo pink into teal" defines the palette precisely. "35mm slide film with a slight red-magenta cast" gives the photographic colour, and "not a poster or illustration" avoids flat vector sunsets.
