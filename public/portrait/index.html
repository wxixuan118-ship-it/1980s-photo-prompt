How to write a convincing 1980s portrait prompt

Start with the backdrop. School-picture blues, marbled grey canvas and laser-beam backgrounds place a portrait in the decade before the viewer even looks at the face.

Then decide on the light. A single on-camera flash gives hard shadows and bright skin; a big soft box gives the glossy glamour-shot finish of mall photo studios.

Finally, keep the pose simple. Chin slightly down, shoulders angled, eyes straight to the lens: stiff, friendly and a little formal is exactly how most 80s portraits looked.
