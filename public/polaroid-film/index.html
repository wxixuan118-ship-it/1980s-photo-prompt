How to make a Polaroid photo prompt look like real film

Say which camera took the shot. Polaroid 600, a disposable point-and-shoot or a cheap 35mm compact each produce a different mix of flash, sharpness and color.

Ask for the flaws: slight overexposure, dust, a warm or green color cast, uneven borders and a little motion blur. Perfect images are the giveaway of AI.

Avoid modern quality words. Terms like 8K, ultra detailed or HDR fight directly against the soft, grainy texture you are trying to recreate.
