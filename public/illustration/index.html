Featured illustration prompts

  • Aerobics Class With Cake – The joke is instantly readable: four people giving an aerobics routine everything they have while each carefully balances a plate of layered cake.
  • Black Falcon Castle Assault – The prompt aims for a childhood toy-box memory but the result goes further, turning a beloved 80s castle set into a full-scale fantasy battle.
  • Live-Action ThunderCats Heroes – Seeing a Saturday-morning cartoon cast rebuilt with real fur, muscle and leather is instantly compelling.
  • Three Friends at Retro Diner – Three friends squeezed cheek to cheek at a diner counter have a warmth that feels instantly familiar.
  • Purple Glasses Pop Art Woman – Strong ink lines, a tightly controlled purple and turquoise palette and a dotted halftone backdrop give this portrait instant graphic punch.
  • Drag Glamour Sunset Sports Car – Everything here is turned up: a cascade of teased red curls, a highlighter-yellow jacket, a sapphire pendant and a head-back laugh that radiates fun.
  • Synthwave Street Disco Party – A giant disco ball hanging over a rain-slick downtown street is a gloriously unlikely image, and it works. Laser beams slice between skyscrapers, grid-and-sunset billboards glow on both sides, and dancing silhouettes reflect in the wet asphalt.
  • Madonna-Inspired Pop Art Collage – The contrast between the monochrome face and the dripping hot pink paint is what grabs you. A platinum-curled figure tips her head back, eyes closed, as neon pink streaks, dots and white blossoms rain across the frame.

Getting the 1980s illustration style right in a prompt

Name the medium. Airbrush, gouache, cel animation, halftone comic print and early CGI each have a distinct texture that the model can reproduce.

Borrow the palette: hot pink, teal, purple gradients and chrome lettering signal the decade even when the subject is modern.

Reference the format rather than an artist, such as a VHS cover, arcade flyer or Saturday-morning cartoon title card, to keep results original.
